# NovaMotion purchase-draft process history

**Status: proposed.** First read-only increment for [#22](https://github.com/erp4us-org/erp4us/issues/22) and [WFL-001](../requirements/catalog/WFL.yaml) in [First Working ERP #119](https://github.com/erp4us-org/erp4us/issues/119) / [draft PR #120](https://github.com/erp4us-org/erp4us/pull/120). Evidence below describes the draft checkpoint [fff2d77](https://github.com/erp4us-org/erp4us/commit/fff2d77), not production or completed acceptance of #22.

## What the coordinator needs

On fresh NovaMotion stock, Atlas orders ten `NM-1000/A`. Inventory records zero finished units reserved and ten outstanding, saves a pinned material plan and reserves available inputs. The coordinator sends the uncovered quantities: 12 end plates, 6 motors, 10 spindles, 8 rails and 10 sensors. The buyer should see that one draft has actually been recorded, or know that the handoff was observed and the draft is not yet visible. Neither a recommendation nor a broker acknowledgement authorizes spend. By contrast, a consultancy selling engineering time has no stock reservation or material plan. Its order should not enter or appear stalled in this physical-line history. Service-only Sales is a design counterexample, not an implemented flow at this checkpoint: restrict the proof to today's physical Sales path, and require an owner-defined eligibility signal before expanding it rather than inferring line type from a SKU or missing reservation.

Use a small durable projection owned by the [baseline Workflow boundary](../architecture/baseline.md), not an orchestrator. An alternative is to compose existing owner GETs under the same actor at request time. Those reads are useful for current detail, but a late or unavailable response cannot reconstruct past transitions or distinguish an unobserved handoff from a missing causal predecessor. The projection earns its extra persistence by surviving interruptions and replay. Keep owner reads as the existing fallback if this proof cannot be made safely; do not present composed current state as history.

## Owner facts and proposed contracts

Only two steps already publish to JetStream on the real preview path. The other three facts are committed today but need new owner events to become independent, replayable history. The fixture-only shortage and draft adapters are not evidence of live publication.

Define language-neutral JSON Schemas, independent examples and cross-field checks before writing adapters. Use the [shared transport](../specifications/nats-messaging.md): full subject `v1.business.<tenant-uuid>.<domain>.event.<resource>.<action>.v1`, headers and envelope with exact protocol/schema `1.0.0` initially, explicitly tested compatibility bounds, and `erp4us-messaging` in Rust. A new transition has a fresh persisted `messageId` and preserves the root correlation; `causationId` points to the actual triggering owner fact as above. Validate scope and the order/line/plan relationships as well as individual envelopes. Keep the new outbox entries in the owner transaction; retry relay publishes with the same ID and only mark them sent after broker acknowledgement. A command retry, equivalent delivery, or lookup emits no new business transition.

## Persist, order and recover

- **Store only what the view needs.** Workflow owns an inbox, projection and recovery checkpoint in its own namespace. Key runs by `(tenant, organization, order, line)` and check the Sales correlation against that run; it is not an access credential or a substitute for the business key. Keep typed codes, necessary references and revisions, event/correlation/parent IDs, owner, wire versions, event time and local first-observed time. Persist deduplication and projection effect (or a staged fact) atomically before ACK. Read one consistent snapshot. No cross-service DB reads or writes.
- **Causation before time.** Show the proved chain in business order, using the typed plan prerequisite for the existing handoff. For independent facts use owner event time in UTC and message ID as a stable tie-breaker, not broker sequence or receive order. If draft arrives before handoff, or handoff before plan, stage it without advancing the visible chain. Re-evaluate when missing predecessors arrive. A known gap yields `incomplete` once the root exists; without a visible root there is no history response. Do not treat holes in the shared stream sequence as process gaps.
- **Redelivery and conflicts.** Match the scoped original message ID, not `Nats-Msg-Id` or delivery sequence. Also enforce one logical owner transition per scoped resource/revision (e.g. one handoff and one draft per plan). Compare validated, canonical content, using a restricted versioned fingerprint rather than retaining bodies. Equivalent facts under fresh IDs can be recorded as duplicate receipts, not new steps or substitute parents; matching only on correlation is insufficient. Changed content under the same message ID, contradictory facts for one logical transition, wrong parent/scope/IDs, or unsupported versions must not advance a run. Keep a minimal quarantine reference and bounded reason; do not let an invalid foreign envelope alter a legitimate run. For an authorized conflicting fact, freeze the affected branch as incomplete rather than choosing by arrival time.
- **Retry and rebuild.** Retry transient DB or broker errors with bounded backoff; persist quarantine before terminal ACK for invalid or exhausted input. Keep unresolved valid dependencies in bounded staging and surface an incomplete observation rather than synthesizing a predecessor. Restart with the same durable and persisted inbox. Prove replay or rebuild from retained original envelopes into an isolated projection, not by reissuing a business command. The preview business stream [has limits](../../services/article-delivery/src/main.rs): 30 days, 32 MiB, discard new on capacity; it is not an archive. If a required event is outside retention, rely on a verified projection backup or an owner-authorized source that preserves the original identity, if available; otherwise keep the gap. No guaranteed historical backfill or new repair endpoint.

Report the greatest verified prefix and a stable reason:

| State | Evidence |
|---|---|
| `waiting` | Name the next unobserved step: reservation, plan, handoff or draft. Missing planning or send evidence is not failure or proof no one acted. |
| `no_purchase_needed` | A full reservation, or a saved plan with no positive buy rows. Neither implies the order is ready to ship. |
| `stalled` | A handoff was observed, no draft fact has arrived within the preview threshold (propose 30 seconds from persisted first observation), and the projection's exact filters/durables have a fresh caught-up measurement. This is observational only. |
| `incomplete` | A known dependency gap/conflict, or inability to establish a caught-up observation when evaluating a possible stall. Missing or stale measurements and outstanding projection deliveries are not zero. |
| `draft_recorded` | The verified prefix includes the matching draft; this is not a completed order or approval. |

Test the measurement freshness bound and invalidate on disconnect. Do not infer quarantine causes from platform counters; neither today's diagnostics nor the owner quarantine records are an order-level outcome feed. A normal worker restart and replay can clear a wait or stall without a new operator command.

## A scoped GET and browser view

Proposed `GET /v1/workflow/orders/{order_id}/lines/{line_id}/process-history`. Use bearer authentication and the existing `X-Erp4us-Tenant` / `X-Erp4us-Organization` selectors. Workflow verifies credential, actor, exact membership and a proposed `workflow.process-history.read` operation grant. Require **all** participating owner read grants for the selected scope: `sales.order.read`, `inventory.reservation.read`, `inventory.material-plan.read`, `procurement.proposal.read`. This is an all-or-nothing view, not a privilege derived from an owner grant or a platform operator role; even an early history can reveal downstream existence through its status.

Constrain lookup to both verified scope fields and the exact order/line; all linked plan and proposal entities must be visible to that actor under owner policy. For the synthetic preview the owners' reads are organization-wide given the exact operation grant and a matching scoped entity. If narrower entity policy applies, require an authoritative current owner visibility decision under the same actor before returning history or status; if that cannot be enforced, do not offer the route for that scope. Missing evidence is not permission, and scope headers, IDs, manifests or browser checks never grant access. Authorize on every request, equally for human, service and agent actors. Do not return partial steps, counts or timings as an existence hint to a caller missing access.

Example `200` for an observed handoff after the stall threshold:

```json
{
  "state": "stalled",
  "reason": "draft_not_observed",
  "steps": [
    {"kind": "order_confirmed", "occurred_at": "2026-11-27T09:00:00Z"},
    {"kind": "reservation_recorded", "outcome": "none", "occurred_at": "2026-11-27T09:00:02Z"},
    {"kind": "material_plan_saved", "occurred_at": "2026-11-28T07:55:00Z"},
    {"kind": "handoff_observed", "occurred_at": "2026-11-28T08:00:00Z"}
  ]
}
```

The step time is the owner event time, not a global sequence or a freshness promise. Return at most five steps, `Cache-Control: no-store`, and fixed codes/strings only. `400` malformed selectors or IDs; `401` invalid or absent credentials; `403` denied scope or operation before entity disclosure; indistinguishable `404` for absent, foreign, hidden or not-yet-projected history in an otherwise authorized query; `503` when the authorized view cannot be read safely because its store or a required authorization dependency is unavailable. No list, export, raw event lookup or mutation. Do not expose actor IDs, correlation or tracing IDs, subjects, sequences, quarantine reasons, raw bodies, customer/partner details, suppliers, terms, prices, BOM or stock quantities. Logs and quarantine use allowlisted reason/reference fields without bodies, credentials or PII.

Start from the existing authorized Sales order line. Render translated step/status labels and a read-only refresh, with a translated label and decorative icon for the native button. Clear the view on scope or session change and discard in-flight responses for the old scope. On a stall, explain what was last observed and direct authorized staff to existing support or owner views, not an admin replay button. This is distinct from the platform diagnostics screen.

## Proof and review gates

1. **Contract and data proof.** Sales, Inventory and Procurement owners review publication points, IDs and data minimization. Validate the two published fixtures and three proposed schemas/examples, root/parent and version rules, header/envelope equality and payload redaction. In the projection feed a reverse-order chain with skewed clocks, a duplicate after ACK loss, equivalent facts with a new delivery/message ID, conflicting revisions and wrong-scope parents. Assert one ordered history, no fabricated steps, unchanged original IDs and a persistent incomplete result for a missing predecessor. Restore/rebuild and replay with and without the retained predecessor. Inventory and Procurement event/outbox proof, projection fixtures, and API/browser contract tests can proceed in parallel once the proposed wire shapes and visibility rules are agreed. An in-process fixture can prove the data/contract behavior without provisioning anything.
2. **API, browser and live recovery proof.** Extend the existing [real handoff exercise](../../tests/integration/procurement_handoff.py) and [Sales browser scenario](../../ui/tests/sales-confirmation.spec.ts): run the real ten-unit flow and verify the five exact shortage quantities in owner APIs, with one stable proposal and no extra holds or proposal after retries. Reopen the same projected chain after restart. Stop the Procurement worker before sending the handoff, observe the handoff and `waiting` then the bounded `stalled` state, resume the worker and see `draft_recorded`. Separately stop the broker/relay before the handoff reaches the projector and prove it never shows an observed handoff or fabricated Procurement stall. Interrupt the projection after its durable write and before ACK; restart and replay, asserting no new business effects. Cover fully reserved and make-only cases and an out-of-scope service-only fixture without a fabricated purchasing wait. Use two tenants and two organizations, a wrong audience, missing owner and Workflow grants, an entity not visible to the caller and a platform-only actor. Check status/body/cache redaction and that switching scope cannot retain or flash another history. Use a clean real sign-in session for browser acceptance; mocked UI alone is not proof.
3. **Approval before activation.** There is no deployed Workflow reader or approved grant today. The baseline placement is a target, not approval to introduce a deployable, provision a database/NATS principal, extend existing Inventory or Procurement publish ACLs, or choose retention/recovery, delegation or entity visibility policy. Maintainer and security review must approve verified event provenance (connection principal constrained to owner/tenant routes, organization checked on consumption; `sourceService` is not identity), dedicated least-privilege consumer access, route/grant ownership, data retention/restore and non-local trust assumptions. Do not reuse the diagnostics, provisioner or owner credentials for a Workflow subscriber, or put broker access in the browser. Defer live wiring if these decisions or a non-leaking visibility rule are missing. Any accepted consequential design is recorded via the [RFC process](../../GOVERNANCE.md#rfc-process). Start live proof on fresh synthetic orders after all producers are instrumented; older retained fragments are not proof of absence or completeness. Rollback disables the optional view/consumer without rewriting old events, touching owner state or requiring an owner-wide migration.

This proposal adds no code, services, credentials or dependencies. It does not add approvals, operator mutations, a workflow designer, receipts, compensation or release policy. Ticket #22 and WFL-001 remain open: [#23](https://github.com/erp4us-org/erp4us/issues/23) and owner contracts for later transitions are needed to prove the interrupted receipt and remaining reservation, with authorized recovery that cannot issue a second purchase. Keep runtime/log controls in [#122](https://github.com/erp4us-org/erp4us/issues/122) separate.

The [test-only history proof](../../tests/contracts/process_history_reference.py) can be run with `python3 tests/contracts/process_history_reference.py`. It uses the two published envelope examples and three hypothetical, in-memory owner facts. Its checks demonstrate causal ordering, gap/observation states, replay conflicts, scoped keys and a reduced response shape. They do not verify live producer grants, a broker or database outage, persisted replay, or production retention and recovery.
