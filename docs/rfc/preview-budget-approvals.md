# Staged purchase approvals for the NovaMotion preview

Scope: [#23](https://github.com/erp4us-org/erp4us/issues/23), [PUR-001](../requirements/catalog/PUR.yaml), and [First Working ERP #119](https://github.com/erp4us-org/erp4us/issues/119). The [recorded preview direction](https://github.com/erp4us-org/erp4us/issues/23#issuecomment-7237683084) confirms separate purchasing/receiving authority and multi-level budget approvals. The exact limits, role assignments, and distinction between preview and production rules remain explicit configuration decisions.

**Requirement:** a scoped buyer cannot issue a purchase based on a shortage or draft alone. Approve the complete immutable terms snapshot, then issue through a separate authorized action only when every required stage is satisfied. A changed supplier, price, quantity, or terms/policy revision cannot use the previous approval. The maker of the terms cannot approve that revision in this preview. Recording approval alone takes no stock, does not issue an order and does not contact suppliers.

**Pilot policy shape:** one trusted versioned policy in the verified tenant/organization, with fixed `EUR` and ordered, cumulative, inclusive minimum amounts. The first minimum is zero and additional minimums increase strictly. A stage key `department` requires the exact independently verified operation grant `procurement.approval.department`; the policy file cannot grant it. The policy explicitly states whether stages require different approvers, and does not infer a rule from role names, a buyer request or an unspecified policy. No configured policy or invalid/incompatible policy means approvals are unavailable and issuance is denied, not silently treated as preapproved.

The [policy schema](../../contracts/schemas/procurement-budget-policy-v1.json) and [synthetic example](../../contracts/examples/procurement/budget-policy-v1.json) are a private deployment input, not a caller-controlled setting or an organization admin API. The example is **test data, not an accepted spending limit**: a department approval starts at `0.00`, and a budget approval is also required at `1500.00`. The current NovaMotion `1882.40` draft exercises both; `1499.99` exercises just the first. A solo firm may use a different explicitly approved policy, not an accidental self-approval fallback. A purchase that draws on a real cost-center balance or credit limit needs separate budget availability and reservation rules; this preview tests amount-based approval only.

**Ownership and concurrency:** use the complete saved draft total before any grouping by supplier. Never round, compare binary floats, or accept a currency/total from the approval client. Persist decisions against the scoped proposal, exact terms revision, policy ID/revision and resolved stage requirements, including verified actor and a stable command ID. Preserve old decisions for audit without carrying them to new terms or policy. Serialize edits, approvals and eventual issuance on the same Procurement-owned write guard so an old snapshot cannot be approved for current use or converted into an order by a race. Same intent and command ID replays its original result; altered intent conflicts. An out-of-order stage, wrong grant, cross-scope request, self-approval or policy/terms mismatch never advances approval. No cross-service writes; Inventory owns receipts and allocation under the separate receiving grant and rejects over-receipts.

**Proof:** check zero, just below and exactly at each boundary; a two-stage draft is not approved after the first stage. Test denied self-approval, missing grants, wrong organization, distinct-stage actor rule when enabled, stale revisions, policy absence/change, concurrent edits/decisions, stable replay and restart. Add the scoped API and authenticated browser proof before any issuance. Purchasing approval is not budget reservation, purchase order transmission, receipt, or shipment.

The [local issuance and receipt design](preview-purchase-issuance.md) follows this approval gate and keeps the two business writes in their owning services.
